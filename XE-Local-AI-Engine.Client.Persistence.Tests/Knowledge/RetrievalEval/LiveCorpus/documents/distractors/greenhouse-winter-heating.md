# Heating a hobby greenhouse in winter

A hobby greenhouse that has to stay frost free needs a heater, a thermostat and a good layer of insulation. Watering
is the least of the worries in winter; plants in a cold greenhouse need very little water.

## Heater choice

An electric fan heater with a frost-protection setting is the simplest option for a small greenhouse. Paraffin
heaters work without a power line but release water vapour, which encourages grey mould on the plants.

## Insulation

Bubble wrap fixed to the inside of the glass cuts heat loss by about a third. Leave the roof vents clear, because
even in winter a sunny day can overheat a closed greenhouse by noon.

## Thermostat placement

Hang the thermostat at plant height in the middle of the greenhouse, away from the heater and out of direct sun.
A minimum and maximum thermometer next to it shows whether the night temperature really stayed above freezing.
