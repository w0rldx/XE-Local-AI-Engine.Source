# Indexing a rear derailleur

When the chain hesitates between sprockets, the rear derailleur needs indexing. The chain itself is rarely the
problem; the cable tension is.

## Limit screws first

Before touching the barrel adjuster, check that the high and low limit screws stop the chain from dropping off the
smallest sprocket or into the spokes. Shift through the whole cassette with the chain on the middle chainring.

## Cable tension

Shift to the second-smallest sprocket. If the chain rubs or hesitates on the way up the cassette, turn the barrel
adjuster a quarter turn anticlockwise to add cable tension. If it hesitates on the way down, turn it clockwise.

## Hanger alignment

If indexing never feels right across the whole cassette, the derailleur hanger is probably bent. A hanger alignment
gauge shows it quickly; a bent hanger makes the chain run crooked on every sprocket.
