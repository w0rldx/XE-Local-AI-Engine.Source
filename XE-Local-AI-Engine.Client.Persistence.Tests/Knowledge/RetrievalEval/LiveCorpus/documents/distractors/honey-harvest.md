# Harvesting honey

Honey is ready when the bees have capped most of the cells on a frame. Uncapped honey holds too much water and will
ferment in the jar.

## Clearing the supers

A clearer board placed under the honey supers the evening before lets the bees move down into the brood box. The next
morning the supers are nearly empty of bees and can be taken off calmly.

## Extraction

The cappings are cut off with a warm knife, and the frames spin in the extractor, first gently on one side, then
fully on the other. The honey runs through a double sieve into a settling tank and rests for a few days.

## After the harvest

Every colony loses its winter stores at harvest, so feeding with sugar syrup starts right away. The mite count and
any varroa treatment belong to the weeks after the last honey harvest, before the bees raise the winter brood.
