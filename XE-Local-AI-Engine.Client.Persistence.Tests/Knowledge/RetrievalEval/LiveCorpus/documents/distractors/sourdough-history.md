# A short history of sourdough bread

Sourdough is the oldest way of leavening bread. Long before commercial yeast, every baker kept a starter of flour
and water and fed it daily, and the starter feeding was part of the rhythm of a bakery.

## Rye regions

In northern and eastern Europe rye flour dominated, and rye bread needs the acidity of sourdough to bake properly.
Rye starter traditions from these regions still shape the dark breads sold there today.

## The gold rush

Miners in nineteenth-century California carried their sourdough starter with them, and a sourdough starter kept
warm in a pouch became a symbol of the prospector. The feeding ratio and timing were learned by feel, not by weight.

## Industrial yeast

Compressed baker's yeast spread in the late nineteenth century and made bread faster and more predictable. Sourdough
survived in home kitchens and small bakeries and returned to fashion in the early twenty-first century.
