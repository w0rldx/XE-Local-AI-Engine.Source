# Servicing a quartz watch

A quartz watch keeps time with a small quartz crystal that vibrates at 32768 times per second. It needs far less
care than a mechanical watch, but it is not maintenance free.

## Battery change

When the seconds hand starts jumping in four-second steps, the battery is nearly empty. Change it promptly: an old
battery can leak and destroy the quartz movement. After a battery change, the gasket of the case back should be
greased so the watch stays water resistant.

## Manual time setting

After a battery change the time is set by hand with the crown. Some quartz movements have a reset key: pressing a
contact on the movement triggers a manual reset of the circuit before the new battery is inserted.

## Group service

Watchmakers usually collect quartz watches for a group service run once a week, because the pressure test for
water resistance runs several cases in one chamber.
