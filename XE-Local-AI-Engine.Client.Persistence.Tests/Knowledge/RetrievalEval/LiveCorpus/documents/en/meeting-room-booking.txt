Meeting room booking rules

Rooms are booked through the shared calendar. A booking that nobody checks in to within fifteen minutes of its start
is released automatically so someone else can use the room.

The large room on the third floor seats twenty and has the only video bar with two cameras. It can be booked for at
most three hours at a time, and recurring bookings for it need approval from the office manager.

Small phone booths cannot be booked at all; they are first come, first served, with a soft limit of thirty minutes.

Please leave the whiteboard clean and return the remote to its charging dock. Catering for meetings with more than
eight guests must be ordered two working days in advance.
