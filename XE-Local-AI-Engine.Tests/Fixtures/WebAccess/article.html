Tidal Energy Arrives in the Harbour

By A. Reporter

The first tidal turbine in the harbour began feeding the town grid on Monday morning, after four years of planning and two winters of delays caused by storms that damaged the anchoring platform twice.

Engineers say the turbine produces enough electricity for roughly six hundred homes when the tide runs at full strength, and that output is predictable to the minute because tides follow the moon rather than the weather.

Local fishermen, who had worried about the effect on the crab grounds, will take part in a monitoring programme that counts catches in the surrounding bay every month for the next three years.

The council expects to decide on a second turbine next spring, once the first full year of generation data is in.
