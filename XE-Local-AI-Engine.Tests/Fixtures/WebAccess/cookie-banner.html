Sourdough starter in seven days

Mix fifty grams of wholemeal flour with fifty grams of lukewarm water in a clean jar, cover it loosely and leave it somewhere warm. On the second day you may see a few bubbles; that is the wild yeast waking up.

Each day after that, discard half of the mixture and feed what remains with another fifty grams of flour and water. The discard can go into pancakes, so nothing is wasted.

By the end of the week the starter should double in size within six hours of a feed and smell pleasantly sour. That is the sign it is strong enough to raise a loaf.
